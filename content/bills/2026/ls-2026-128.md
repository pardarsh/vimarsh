The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026 amends the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956 to raise the maximum number of judges in the Supreme Court from thirty-three to thirty-seven, excluding the Chief Justice of India. It has retrospective effect from May 16, 2026. The bill also repeals the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026, while preserving any actions taken under it.

## Summary

- Amends Section 2 of the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956 to increase the statutory limit of Supreme Court judges (excluding the Chief Justice of India) from thirty-three to thirty-seven.
- Provides that the amendment is deemed to have taken effect retrospectively on May 16, 2026.
- Repeals the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026 (Ord. 1 of 2026).
- Contains a savings clause validating any actions taken under the principal Act as amended by the repealed Ordinance.

- Clause 3(1): Formally repeals the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026.
- Clause 3(2): Retains legal validity and continuity for any actions taken or decisions made under the 1956 Act as amended by the 2026 Ordinance.
