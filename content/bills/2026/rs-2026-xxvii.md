# The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (Amendment) Bill, 2026

> This analysis was generated automatically by a language model from the official bill text. It may contain errors. Read the official text before relying on it.

The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (Amendment) Bill, 2026 protects in-service school teachers appointed before the commencement of the principal Act from retrospective qualification requirements. It establishes that passing tests like the Teacher Eligibility Test applies only prospectively to appointments made after the Act came into force. Consequently, pre-existing teachers are protected from compulsory retirement, termination, or loss of promotional and retirement benefits solely due to missing these additional qualifications.

## Summary

- Amends Section 23 of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 to exempt teachers appointed before the Act's commencement from acquiring newly notified qualifications for service continuity, promotions, seniority, or terminal benefits.
- Omits the second proviso of Section 23(2) of the principal Act, which previously required teachers in position as of March 31, 2015, to acquire minimum qualifications within four years of the 2017 amendment.
- Mandates that the requirement to pass tests or acquire additional qualifications shall operate prospectively, applying only to appointments made after the principal Act commenced or after a date notified by the appropriate government.
- Prohibits compulsory retirement, termination, denial of promotion, or any other adverse service consequences against pre-existing teachers solely for failing or not acquiring the prescribed test or qualifications.
- Directs the appropriate government to formulate guidelines for the professional upgradation, training, and capacity building of pre-existing teachers, with an explicit restriction that such measures cannot be linked to service security or promotional eligibility.
- Inserts Section 23A with overriding effect to ensure that the service conditions, promotional avenues, and retirement benefits of pre-existing teachers cannot be altered to their disadvantage by introducing new qualification requirements.
- Amends Section 38 to authorize the appropriate government to make rules for framing guidelines on training and capacity building for pre-existing teachers.
- Inserts Section 39A to empower the appropriate government to issue protective directions, clarifications, or guidelines to shield pre-existing teachers from adverse service outcomes.

## Key provisions

- Clause 2: Amends Section 23(1) and omits the second proviso in Section 23(2) of the 2009 Act.
- Clause 2: Inserts sub-sections (4), (5), (6), and (7) into Section 23 to protect pre-existing teachers from being required to acquire additional qualifications, ensure prospective application of tests like TET, forbid adverse service actions, and mandate non-punitive professional training.
- Clause 3: Inserts Section 23A, overriding other laws to protect the service conditions, promotion avenues, and terminal benefits of pre-existing teachers from being adversely changed by new qualification requirements.
- Clause 4: Amends Section 38(2) by adding clause (la) to grant rule-making powers for training and professional upgradation guidelines.
- Clause 5: Inserts Section 39A granting the appropriate government power to issue directions, clarifications, or guidelines to prevent adverse service consequences for pre-existing teachers.

## Fiscal or legal impact

Legally, the bill gives prospective-only effect to teacher eligibility tests and qualification norms under the RTE Act, overriding any contrary laws, rules, or judicial interpretations that mandated retrospective compliance for pre-existing teachers, thereby insulating their tenure, promotion, and retirement benefits from adverse consequences. The bill contains no Financial Memorandum, and the text specifies no direct fiscal appropriation or expenditure.
