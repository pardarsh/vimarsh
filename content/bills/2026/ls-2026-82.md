The Appropriation (No. 2) Act, 2026 authorises the expenditure and withdrawal of specified funds from the Consolidated Fund of India for the financial year 2026-27. It provides statutory backing for government spending across various ministries, departments, and services, encompassing both voted amounts and charged expenditures. The total authorised appropriation amounts to Rs. 162,84,130,06,00,000.

## Summary

- Authorises withdrawal from the Consolidated Fund of India for the financial year 2026-27 up to an aggregate limit of Rs. 162,84,130,06,00,000 (Clause 2).
- Allocates the withdrawn funds across revenue and capital accounts for 102 specific votes and various charged services detailed in the Schedule (Clause 3).
- Provides for Rs. 49,25,513,62,00,000 in expenditures voted by Parliament and Rs. 1,13,58,616,44,00,000 in expenditures charged directly on the Consolidated Fund.
- Accounts for major expenditure categories, including national debt repayments (Rs. 95,34,445,88,00,000) and interest payments (Rs. 14,43,971,79,00,000).
- Clarifies that references to ministries or departments reflect their structures as of July 6, 2021, or as subsequently reconstituted (Clause 4).

## Key provisions

- Clause 2: Grants legal authority to pay and apply up to Rs. 162,84,130,06,00,000 from the Consolidated Fund of India to cover expenses arising during FY 2026-27.
- Clause 3: Mandates that the drawn funds be appropriated exclusively for the specific purposes and services outlined in the Schedule.
- Clause 4: Sets the interpretive rule for department and ministry designations, aligning references to structures existing before July 6, 2021, or as reconstituted thereafter.
- The Schedule: Itemises the permissible upper limits for revenue and capital expenditures across individual government ministries, central departments, Union Territories without legislatures, constitutional bodies, and debt obligations.

## Fiscal or legal impact

Legally, the Act satisfies the constitutional mandate under Article 114 of the Constitution of India, providing the central government with the legal authority to withdraw money from the Consolidated Fund of India. Fiscally, it sanctions aggregate payments of Rs. 162,84,130.06 crore for the financial year 2026-27, with Rs. 49,25,513.62 crore voted by Parliament and Rs. 1,13,58,616.44 crore charged on the Consolidated Fund (heavily comprising debt repayment and interest obligations).
