The Appropriation (No. 3) Act, 2026 authorises the appropriation of funds from the Consolidated Fund of India to cover excess expenditure incurred during the financial year ended March 31, 2023. It regularises spending beyond the previously granted amounts for specific services, totalling over Rs. 54,067 crore. The regularised amounts primarily cover debt repayment and Ministry of Railways capital expenditure.

## Summary

- Regularises excess expenditure incurred during the financial year 2022–2023 that exceeded the originally granted appropriations.
- Deems an aggregate sum of Rs. 54,067,45,93,231 to have been authorised and paid out of the Consolidated Fund of India (Section 2).
- Formally appropriates these funds toward specified capital charges charged on the Consolidated Fund (Section 3 and Schedule).
- Allocates Rs. 53,871,00,96,887 to charged capital expenditure for Repayment of Debt (Vote 40).
- Allocates Rs. 196,44,96,344 to charged capital expenditure for the Ministry of Railways (Vote 85).

- Section 1: Defines the short title of the legislation as the Appropriation (No. 3) Act, 2026.
- Section 2: Grants retrospective authorisation to pay and apply Rs. 54,067,45,93,231 out of the Consolidated Fund of India for excess expenditure in the financial year ended March 31, 2023.
- Section 3: Declares that the authorised sums are deemed appropriated for the specific services and purposes listed in the Schedule for the financial year ended March 31, 2023.
- Schedule: Specifies the distribution of the excess charged capital amounts across Vote 40 (Repayment of Debt) and Vote 85 (Ministry of Railways).
