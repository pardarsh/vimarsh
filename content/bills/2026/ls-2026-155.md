# THE KERALA (ALTERATION OF NAME) BILL, 2026

The Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 changes the official name of the State of Kerala to the State of Keralam. It updates relevant constitutional provisions and schedules to reflect this change from an appointed date. The bill also provides transitional legal mechanisms for adapting existing laws and continuing pending legal proceedings under the new name.

## Summary

- Formally alters the name of the State of Kerala to the State of Keralam starting on an appointed day set by the Central Government via the Official Gazette.
- Amends Articles 31A and 290A of the Constitution of India by replacing the word 'Kerala' with 'Keralam'.
- Updates the First Schedule (list of States) and the Fourth Schedule (allocation of Rajya Sabha seats) of the Constitution to replace 'Kerala' with 'Keralam'.
- Empowers the appropriate Government (Central or State) to modify, adapt, or amend pre-existing laws within one year to reflect the name change.
- Grants courts, tribunals, and authorities the power to construe laws consistently with the name change even if formal statutory adaptation orders have not been made.
- Ensures that in all pending legal proceedings where the State of Kerala is a party, the State of Keralam is deemed to be substituted automatically.

## Key provisions

- Clause 1(2): States that the Act takes effect on a date appointed by the Central Government by notification in the Official Gazette.
- Clause 3: Mandates that on and from the appointed day, the State of Kerala shall be known as the State of Keralam.
- Clause 4: Substitutes 'Keralam' for 'Kerala' in Article 31A(2)(a)(i) of the Constitution.
- Clause 5: Substitutes 'Keralam' for 'Kerala' in Article 290A of the Constitution.
- Clause 6: Updates Entry 5 under 'I. THE STATES' in the First Schedule of the Constitution from 'Kerala' to 'Keralam'.
- Clause 7: Updates Entry 9 in the Table of the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution from 'Kerala' to 'Keralam'.
- Clause 8: Authorises the appropriate Government to adapt and modify existing laws within one year of the appointed day, without restricting legislative competence to repeal or amend.
- Clause 9: Empowers courts, tribunals, and authorities to construe unadapted laws in a manner that gives effect to the name change without altering their substance.
- Clause 10: Deems the State of Keralam substituted for the State of Kerala in any legal proceedings pending immediately before the appointed day.

## Fiscal or legal impact

The bill carries significant legal effects by modifying constitutional provisions (Articles 31A, 290A, First Schedule, and Fourth Schedule) and transferring all legal obligations and standing in pending court proceedings from the State of Kerala to the State of Keralam. It allows a one-year window for administrative adaptation of existing statutes. The bill text contains no Financial Memorandum or statements indicating direct fiscal expenditure.
