# THE NATIONAL CO-OPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2026

> This analysis was generated automatically by a language model from the official bill text. It may contain errors. Read the official text before relying on it.

The National Co-operative Development Corporation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 updates the National Co-operative Development Corporation Act, 1962 to expand the Corporation's mandate beyond agricultural items to industrial goods and processed foods. It enables direct financing, loans, grants, and equity participation to cooperative societies and intermediary entities engaged in cooperative development. It also allows the Corporation to share credit information with the Reserve Bank of India, the Central Government, and financial institutions.

## Summary

- Amends the National Co-operative Development Corporation Act, 1962 to introduce a formal definition for 'co-operative development' encompassing planning, promoting, and financing programs directly or via intermediary entities.
- Updates references to cooperative law, replacing the repealed Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 1984 with the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002.
- Expands the scope of foodstuffs and goods under the Act to include processed foods, other edible products, other notified food items, and industrial goods.
- Authorizes the Corporation to provide loans and grants directly to cooperative societies or entities engaged in cooperative development, subject to security requirements.
- Allows the Corporation to participate in the share capital of cooperatives or cooperative development entities with Central Government approval.
- Permits the Corporation to exchange credit information and other data with the Central Government, the Reserve Bank of India, and specified banking and financial institutions.

## Key provisions

- Clause 2: Defines 'co-operative development' under section 2; updates statutory references to the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002; widens the definition of foodstuffs to include processed foods and other notified edible items; and broadens cottage industries to encompass handicrafts or other crafts generally.
- Clause 3 and Clause 4: Omits section 2A and sub-section (4)(vii) of section 3 of the principal Act.
- Clause 5: Amends section 9 to expand Corporation functions to include industrial goods; allows funding to State Governments for financing cooperatives or development entities; inserts clause (da) to provide direct loans and grants to cooperatives and development entities; inserts clause (g) to allow equity participation in such entities with Central Government approval; and provides that eligible entities engaged in cooperative development will be determined by the Board.
- Clause 6: Inserts section 9A, granting the Corporation incidental and consequential powers to perform its duties under the Act or other applicable laws.
- Clause 7: Inserts section 13A, permitting the Corporation to collect and furnish credit information to and from the Central Government, the Reserve Bank, and notified banking or financial institutions.

## Fiscal or legal impact

Legally, the bill broadens the operational and financing scope of the National Co-operative Development Corporation under the 1962 Act, allowing it to finance entities through direct lending, grant mechanisms, and equity participation, and legally empowers it to exchange credit information with financial regulators and banks. The bill text itself contains no Financial Memorandum or specified appropriation figures regarding direct fiscal outlays from the Consolidated Fund of India.
