# THE ANDHRA PRADESH REORGANISATION (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2026

The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 amends the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 to formally designate Amaravati as the capital of the State of Andhra Pradesh. It gives retrospective legal effect to this designation starting from June 2, 2024. The bill also defines Amaravati by reference to the capital city areas notified under the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority Act, 2014.

## Summary

- Amends Section 5 of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 regarding the capital of Andhra Pradesh.
- Replaces the generic phrase 'and there shall be a new capital' with 'and Amaravati shall be the new capital' in Section 5(2).
- Adds an Explanation clarifying that 'Amaravati' includes the capital city areas notified under the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority Act, 2014.
- Applies the amendment retrospectively, deeming it to have come into force on June 2, 2024.

## Key provisions

- Clause 1(2): Provides retrospective commencement, stating the Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 2nd day of June, 2024.
- Clause 2(a): Amends Section 5(2) of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 to explicitly designate Amaravati as the new capital of Andhra Pradesh.
- Clause 2(b): Numbers the existing Explanation to Section 5 as Explanation 1 and inserts Explanation 2, defining 'Amaravati' to include the capital city areas notified under the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority Act, 2014.

## Fiscal or legal impact

Legally, the bill establishes Amaravati as the statutory capital of Andhra Pradesh under central reorganisation legislation with retrospective effect from June 2, 2024, referencing areas notified under the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority Act, 2014. The bill text contains no Financial Memorandum or explicit statement regarding fiscal expenditure.
